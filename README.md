# Sunny Side Bakery

A short, cozy browser game. Grandma Rose threw out her back, so you run her little Main Street bakery for five days.

- Tap dough to put it in an oven, tap the oven when it glows golden, then tap a customer to hand over their order.
- Each day lasts about a minute. Earn stars, upgrade the shop, and uncover the story of Mr. Walter and the yellow roses.
- Works on desktop and phones. Progress is saved in the browser.

Two more ways to play, linked from the title screen:

- **Secret Recipe Hunt** (`recipe.html`): Old Gus has vanished and left his recipe pages around town. Visit the mill, apiary, dairy, market, orchard, and post office, solve puzzles (fractions, a bee dance, a yeast experiment, a churning rhythm, a market budget, recipe scaling, a balance scale, and step ordering), and bake three lost recipes.
- **Bake Buddies** (`merge.html`): drag ingredients together to merge them (two wheat make flour, flour and water make dough, and so on) until you meet all 8 bread buddies. Deliver buddies to customers for coins.

Play: open `index.html`, or enable GitHub Pages for this repository.

Character art: `img/` holds each character in three expressions (`-happy`, `-mid`, `-sad`) plus the ending scene.
